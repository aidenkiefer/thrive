## Our History

### In the Beginning…

Co-Pastors Kevin and Molly Kiefer remember that evening vividly. They were playing ping-pong in their basement after the kids were in bed. At the time, they were the parents of three elementary school-age children. Molly was a stay-home mom and Kevin had been on staff at the Elgin Vineyard Church for 8 years.

“I think God is calling us to take a risky next step,” Kevin said as he crushed the ball past Molly’s head. “Yes,” Molly said. “What if took the best of what we have learned over the years and what if we could bring God’s love and power to a new city? Do you think we could see His Kingdom impact another suburb through a new church plant in the area? As Vineyard founder, John Wimber always said, faith is spelled, R-I-S-K.”

Then August of 2009, with the full support and blessings of the leaders of the Elgin Vineyard, Kevin and Molly gathered a small group of friends and families in the living room of their home in Palatine, IL, to share their vision for a local church that would carry God’s love to impact a new city.

Before long, the fledgling congregation outgrew their tiny living room, and met for the next few years on Saturday nights at Christ Lutheran Church, before moving to Cutting Hall in downtown Palatine on Sunday mornings.

In 2016, Thrive Vineyard Church moved into its current home at 845 E. Glencoe St, Palatine, IL 60074, with a 170-seat auditorium upstairs and the children’s ministry classrooms and cafe downstairs...
