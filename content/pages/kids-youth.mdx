# Kids

## Kids are the Future!

We partner with parents, creating safe places where kids can grow in their faith and encounter Jesus.

Let’s establish the foundation for a lifelong friendship with Jesus together!!

### The Nest

### Lil Buds

Arrive a few minutes early to check your child into their classroom for the duration of the service. If you want to keep them with you for worship and then bring them down for the service, that's an option as well.

### Treehouse

School-aged kids have their own space to work through a Bible lesson, have a snack, and watch an application video. It’s an age-appropriate opportunity for kids to discover who God is and how great He made them. Parents will be dismissed to check their elementary-aged child in to Treehouse after family worship.
