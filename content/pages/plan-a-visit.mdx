# Plan Your Visit

We can't wait to meet you. Here's everything you need to know for your first Sunday at Thrive Vineyard.

## Our Services

We offer two Sunday morning services:

| Service | Time | Location | Length |
|---------|------|----------|--------|
| **Express(o)** | 8:30 AM | Thrive Cafe (downstairs) | ~40–45 min |
| **Family Worship** | 9:30 AM | Sanctuary (upstairs) | ~80–90 min |

> **Note:** The Express(o) service is currently paused while we complete building renovations. We look forward to welcoming you back soon! In the meantime, please join us for Family Worship at 9:30 AM.

**Express(o) Service** — A shorter, more intimate gathering with coffee and muffins. Includes a message, communion, and prayer. No worship band. Great for early risers or those with limited time.

**Family Worship** — Our full Sunday service with worship music, preaching, prayer ministry, and kids programs running simultaneously.

## What to Expect

**Step 1: Find Us**
We meet at 845 E Glencoe St, Palatine, IL 60074. Parking is available on-site. Look for the Thrive Vineyard signs.

**Step 2: Arrive a Little Early**
Especially if you have kids — we recommend arriving 10–15 minutes before service to check in your children and get settled.

**Step 3: Check In Your Kids**
We have safe, engaging environments for children from birth through 12th grade. Our team will help you find the right room and complete check-in. Bring a valid photo ID for pickup.

**Step 4: Come As You Are**
No dress code, no pressure. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church home, you belong here. Our greeters will welcome you at the door — and there may even be a gift for first-time visitors!

**Step 5: Stay Connected**
After the service, stick around and meet people. Our team would love to hear your story and answer any questions.

## Kids & Youth

We believe kids should love church, not just tolerate it. Here's where children go during Family Worship:

| Room | Ages |
|------|------|
| The Nest | Birth – 2 years |
| Lil Buds | 2 years – Pre-K |
| Treehouse | Kindergarten – 5th grade |
| Thrive Youth Group | 6th – 12th grade |

**Kids check-in:** Please arrive 10–15 minutes before the service to check in your children. A parent or guardian must present a valid ID at pickup.
