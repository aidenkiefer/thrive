## Wrap-up protocol (about 95% of usage)

When an agent nears its limit it finishes the ticket in hand, runs the gate, pushes its branch, writes its session log, and stops starting new tickets. The PM reconciles the board and PR bases, then writes the handoff note. Do not leave uncommitted work or an ungated push.
